Who I Am

I'm a second-year B.Tech Computer Science (AI) student at Manipal Institute of Technology, Bengaluru. My focus is on building full-stack applications that are clean, efficient, and solve real problems — not just writing code that runs.

On the backend I work with Node.js, Express.js, and MySQL — implementing JWT authentication, REST APIs, and relational database design. I've gone through the full lifecycle of a production project: local development, debugging, and deployment on Render and Clever Cloud.
