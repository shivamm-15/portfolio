Who I Am

I'm a B.Tech Computer Science (AI) student at Manipal Institute of Technology, Bengaluru. I like turning everyday problems into working software, and I'm looking for an SDE internship where I can help build real products.

My core is backend and systems work: REST APIs, JWT authentication, and relational database design. With Nexus, I went further into distributed real-time services — WebSockets and Redis pub/sub, server-side lag compensation, stateful reconnection, and a live observability dashboard.

On the applied ML side, I build with PyTorch. NutriVision recognises 15 Indian and 101 global food categories using MobileNetV3 transfer learning, then estimates calories and macros per portion in a Streamlit app.

I've solved 200+ DSA problems across arrays, stacks, trees, BSTs, and heaps, and I take part in LeetCode contests every week. I also competed in HackVega 2.0, a national hackathon with 47,000+ engineering students.

Outside code, I follow financial markets closely — part of why I built StockSim — and I play cricket, which has taught me consistency and teamwork under pressure.

StockSim

A full-stack virtual NSE stock trading platform. Users start with ₹1,00,000 in virtual capital and trade real NSE-listed stocks using live Yahoo Finance prices. Features JWT authentication, weighted average buy-price calculation, real-time portfolio P&L, a net-worth leaderboard, a watchlist, and transaction history, built with MVC architecture on Express.js.

Nexus

A distributed, horizontally-scaled multiplayer game server built from scratch. Matchmaking is decoupled from game hosting and coordinated across instances via Redis pub/sub and TTL-based heartbeat service discovery. Includes an authoritative 20Hz tick simulation, client-side prediction, server-side lag compensation using position-history rewinding, and stateful reconnection with token-based resumption. A load-test script and a live observability dashboard expose tick timing, queue depth, and per-process load.

Live ↗ GitHub ↗

NutriVision

An AI food recognition and calorie estimator. Upload a photo and get top-3 predictions with confidence scores, then portion-based calorie and macro estimates with nutrition charts. Separate MobileNetV3 pipelines handle 15 Indian food categories and 101 global Food-101 categories, with training and evaluation scripts, and a daily intake dashboard. Deployed on Streamlit Community Cloud.
